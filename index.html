Questions about hiring a DJ

Straight answers about the music, the areas I serve and how to book.

Do you DJ Jewish weddings and play Israeli music?

Yes. At Jewish weddings I play Mediterranean music, contemporary Israeli hits and nostalgic Israeli classics, alongside world music. For bar and bat mitzvas I blend local hits with traditional music so every generation gets on the dance floor.

Which areas do you serve?

Seattle, Bellevue, Redmond and the wider Puget Sound area, at the venue of your choice.

What kinds of events do you DJ?

Jewish weddings, other weddings, bar and bat mitzvas, corporate and private parties, holiday and community celebrations, and custom productions such as tribute evenings and themed nights.

What equipment do you bring?

A MacBook Pro, Rane Four controller with Serato DJ, Mackie ProFX8 mixer, QSC K12 speakers and sub, Sennheiser and Shure microphones and headset, plus a fog machine and lights. I arrive at every event fully backed up.

How do I check availability and book?

Call or text me, or send your event date and details through the contact form and I'll get back to you shortly.
